Web Design Steps

Determine the Intended Audiance
The colors, images, fonts, and layout should be tailored to the prefrences of your audience. The type of site content (reading level, amount of animation, etc.) should be appropriate for your chosen audience.
Determine the Goals of the Site
Some common goals of websites are to be informative, to create a personal home page, to establish a corprate Web presence, and to do business in e-commerce.
Determine the Genertal Content and Create a Site Map
The site map is sometimes called the storyboard. Common organizarional structures for websites are: hierarchical, linear, and random.
Sketch a Tentative page layout
Be consistent in your overall layout — color, typfaces, logo location, navigation bar, etc. Plan your home page layout to avoid scrolling.Plan you content page layout to be consistent, with easy navigation.
